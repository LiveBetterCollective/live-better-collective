A few good questions

Wondering where you fit?

Do I need marketing experience?

No. The Collective is designed to help people learn practical skills step by step, starting with their own experiences and interests.

Do I need to buy products to create content?

You can create accurate educational content from approved product information without claiming you've personally used a product. Any personal testimonial must reflect genuine experience, and all claims must follow company policies and applicable advertising rules.

Is this network marketing?

Yes. We teach transferable digital marketing skills inside a community built around a network marketing opportunity. Our business partner is Zilara. You can review the model, products, compensation, and requirements before deciding whether to participate.

Does it cost money to get started?

Our business partner offers a free affiliate option. Team education is offered without separate course tuition; specific access terms and optional purchases should be confirmed before enrollment. Earning commissions requires qualifying sales.

Will I earn money if I follow the training?

No income is guaranteed. Training can help you develop skills, but outcomes depend on factors including customer demand, effort, expenses, and the business's compensation rules.
